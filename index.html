Leading Causes of Death & Drug Overdose in the U.S.

Comparing major causes of death with overdose trends.

About

We use CDC datasets to see which causes of death are most common, how overdose rates change over time, and who is most affected.

Research Questions

  1. Which leading cause of death has the highest number of deaths nationwide?
  2. Which states have the highest and lowest age-adjusted death rates for the top causes of death?
  3. How have drug overdose death rates changed from 1999 to recent years?
  4. Which drug types show the highest increase in death rates?
  5. Which demographic groups are most impacted by drug overdoses?
  6. Are drug overdose deaths rising faster than other leading causes of death?
  7. What patterns appear between overdose deaths and U.S. regions?

Data Sources

  • Dataset 1 (CDC, CSV): Leading causes of death by year/state.
  • Dataset 2 (CDC, CSV): Overdose death rates by drug type/demographics.
  • Dataset 3 (CT, JSON): Connecticut overdose deaths for comparison.
